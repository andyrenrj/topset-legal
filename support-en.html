Frequently asked questions

  • How do I sign in? Use your email and password, or Sign in with Apple. You can browse programs and exercises and use the calculators without an account.
  • I forgot my password. On the Sign in screen, tap “Forgot password?”. We’ll email you a 6-digit code to set a new one.
  • How do I switch between kg and lb? Go to Profile › Training settings › Weight unit. Plates, history and the calculators convert for you.
  • How do I use dark mode? Go to Profile › Training settings › Appearance and choose System, Light or Dark.
  • How do I change the language? TopSet follows your iPhone’s language (English or Simplified Chinese). To use a different one for TopSet only, open the iPhone Settings app › Apps › TopSet › Language.
  • The rest timer doesn’t show on my lock screen. Make sure Live Activities are on for TopSet in the iPhone Settings app › Apps › TopSet.
  • My workout didn’t sync. Check your internet connection. Sets you log offline are saved on your iPhone and upload automatically when you’re back online. Anything that still needs your attention appears under Pending records on the Today tab.
  • How do I export my data? Go to Profile › Training summary and tap Export CSV.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to Profile › Athlete profile › Account & Security › Delete account. Your plans and training history are deleted with it.
